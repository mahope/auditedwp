# Chapter 8: Templates and Resources

This chapter gives you the templates you need to get compliant quickly. You can
adapt each one to your specific business.

## Template 1: Privacy Policy Checklist

Your privacy policy should cover these sections:

1. **Who we are** — Company name, address, email, VAT/registration number
2. **What data we collect** — List all personal data you collect (name, email, IP,
   cookies, payment data, etc.)
3. **How we collect it** — Forms, cookies, analytics, third-party tools
4. **Why we collect it** — The legal basis for each purpose (consent, contract,
   legitimate interest, legal obligation)
5. **How long we keep it** — Retention periods for each data category
6. **Who we share it with** — Each third-party processor, with links to their privacy
   policies
7. **Where data is stored** — EU/EEA or third countries (with adequacy decision or
   SCCs)
8. **Your rights** — Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection
9. **How to contact us** — Email, address, response time commitment
10. **Complaints** — How to file a complaint with the supervisory authority

## Template 2: Accessibility Statement Structure

Your accessibility statement should include:

1. **Commitment** — "We are committed to making our website accessible to all users..."
2. **Standards** — "We aim to conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA / EN 301 549"
3. **Current status** — "Partially conformant" (if not fully compliant, list what is
   being worked on)
4. **Known limitations** — Honest list of known issues (e.g., "PDFs from 2023 are not
   fully accessible")
5. **Feedback** — Email or form to report issues
6. **Enforcement** — Contact for the national enforcement body
7. **Date** — Date of last review and update

## Template 3: Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Coverage

A DPA with your vendors should cover:

1. **Subject matter and duration** of the processing
2. **Nature and purpose** of the processing
3. **Type of personal data** involved
4. **Categories of data subjects**
5. **Obligations and rights** of the controller
6. **Confidentiality** obligations for the processor
7. **Security measures** the processor must implement
8. **Sub-processor authorization** (general or specific)
9. **Data breach notification** procedures
10. **Data deletion** upon termination
11. **Audit rights** for the controller

## Where to Get Templates

- **EUComply Compliance Store** (eucomplypro.com/store) — Full DPA, NIS2 clauses,
  NDA sets, EAA statement templates
- **European Data Protection Board (EDPB)** — Free guidelines and template documents
- **Your national data protection authority** — Country-specific guidance and templates
- **ICO (UK)** — Good practical guidance even for non-UK businesses
