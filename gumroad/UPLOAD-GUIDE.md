# ComplianceDocs — Gumroad upload package

Everything Mads needs to put the products on sale. **Total time once accounts exist: ~20 minutes.**

## Step 1 — Create the Gumroad account (Mads, 5 min)

1. Go to https://gumroad.com → "Start selling"
2. Sign up with your email, set payout (bank or PayPal)
3. Note the profile URL (e.g. `mahope.gumroad.com`) and write it in STATUS.md
