FAQ

Does the free plugin send my site data anywhere?

No. All checks run server-side on your WordPress installation. No data is sent to EUComply servers, no analytics, no telemetry. Pro license validation is the exception — a simple site-key check against our license server (no site data transmitted).

How is this different from Complianz, CookieYes, or WP Activity Log?

Those plugins solve one compliance problem (cookies, or audit logging). EUComply is the only plugin that combines five compliance checks and generates the legal documentation (DPA, NIS2 clauses, EAA statements, quarterly reports) from the scan results. It's a compliance monitor + document generator in one, not another cookie banner.

Is the generated DPA legally valid?

The DPA follows the standard GDPR Art. 28 structure used across the EU and matches the text of our standalone ComplianceDocs template ($59 sold separately — Pro users get it included). We recommend having a lawyer review the filled-in result for high-value contracts. The template gives you 90% of the work done; legal review is the final 10%.

What happens to my data when I cancel Pro?

The plugin continues working in Free mode — you keep the dashboard and weekly scans. You lose access to document generation, quarterly reports, and email delivery. No data is deleted from your site; it's all stored in your local WordPress database.

Can I white-label the reports for my clients?

Yes. Pro quarterly reports are generated with your agency name (configurable in settings). The PDF footer says "Generated by EUComply" — a white-label upgrade is available for agencies covering 10+ sites. Contact us for details.

Do you cover EAA (European Accessibility Act)?

Yes. The EAA check verifies that an accessibility statement page exists and contains the required disclosures (conformance level, date, contact method). Pro generates a complete EAA statement you can publish immediately. Note: EUComply does not audit actual WCAG conformance — that requires a separate accessibility audit tool.
