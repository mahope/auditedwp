How it works

1 Run a free scan

Enter your URL and get an instant compliance report covering HTTPS, cookie consent, privacy policy links, security headers, and DORA resilience signals.

Scan your website →

2 Copy the badge

Pick the badge type you want — static or dynamic — and paste the code into your site's footer or sidebar. It links back to your compliance scan, free of charge.

3 Re-scan periodically

Compliance is not a one-time event. Run a free re-scan whenever you update your site, change hosting, or add new features. The badge stays accurate as long as you keep monitoring.

Why add a compliance badge?

  • Build trust — Visitors and clients see that your site has been checked against EU requirements
  • Show good faith — Demonstrates to regulators that you are aware of and actively monitoring compliance obligations
  • Free backlink — The badge links back to EUComply, improving your SEO profile
  • No installation — One line of HTML, no JavaScript needed for the static version
  • Works on every platform — WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Next.js, or static HTML
