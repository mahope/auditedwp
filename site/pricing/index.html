Questions

Why is there no buy button?

Because payment is not connected yet, and a button that leads nowhere is worse than none. When Pro opens, this page will say so and the price will be the one listed here.

Will the free scanner get worse when Pro launches?

No. The scanner is finished as it is. Pro adds repetition, history and paperwork on top; it does not take checks away.

Why per website?

Because the cost is per site scanned daily. If you look after many sites, get in touch through mahoje.dk before buying several subscriptions.

Are the templates legal advice?

No. They are documents written by a developer who has had to sign a lot of them, with comments explaining each clause. Use them to arrive at the lawyer with a draft instead of a blank page.

Which currency and payment methods?

Prices are in USD. Payment will run through a merchant of record that handles EU VAT and issues a proper invoice. Cards and PayPal are planned.
