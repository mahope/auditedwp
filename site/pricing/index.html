Questions

What does Pro include today?

The current Pro license unlocks editable HTML starter documents and an HTML report from the latest scan in the WordPress plugin. Hosted daily monitoring is not included.

How is Pro paid for?

Pro costs 79 USD per website per year and is purchased through Stripe. The current purchase does not create a hosted customer account.

Why per website?

Pro is licensed for one website. It is not an unlimited-domain license.

Are the templates legal advice?

No. They are documents written by a developer who has had to sign a lot of them, with comments explaining each clause. Use them to arrive at the lawyer with a draft instead of a blank page.
