Frequently asked questions

Can I use Pro on multiple websites?
Yes — each Pro subscription covers one website. If you manage multiple sites, you can add separate subscriptions per site. Volume pricing is planned for 5+ sites.
What happens after I purchase Pro?
You enter your website URL and we start daily monitoring immediately. You get a dashboard, your first PDF report within minutes, and email alerts whenever the scan detects changes that could affect compliance. All document templates are available right away.
Do I need to install anything?
No. EUComply is a cloud service — you give us your URL, we do the scanning. No code, no plugin, no server access needed. The browser extension is optional and adds in-page scanning.
Can I try Pro before paying?
Absolutely — run the free scanner on your site right now. It runs the same 9-point check. If you find the report useful and want ongoing monitoring, Pro is ready when you are.
What payment methods do you accept?
Credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal — processed through Lemon Squeezy, a Merchant of Record that handles EU VAT and US sales tax for you. You get a proper invoice.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Pro is billed yearly — cancel before the next renewal and you keep access through the end of your current billing period. No lock-in, no early termination fee.
Are the document templates lawyer-approved?
Our templates are created by compliance professionals and reviewed periodically against regulatory changes. They are professional tools, not a substitute for legal advice — we recommend having a qualified attorney review any template before use.
