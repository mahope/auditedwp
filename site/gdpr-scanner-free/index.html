What this free GDPR scanner checks

The scan runs nine automated checks against your live page over plain HTTP/HTML. The ones that map directly to enforcement:

CheckGDPR / ePrivacy link
Cookies set before consentePrivacy Directive: prior consent required for non-essential cookies. This is the single most common finding in EU enforcement against ordinary business sites.
Google Consent Mode v2Required since March 2024 for EEA ad personalisation. Missing signals silently restrict Google Ads measurement.
IAB TCF signalsThe framework most large ad platforms use to receive consent. Missing wiring means a visitor's "Reject" may never reach them.
HTTPS & security headersGDPR Art. 32 requires appropriate technical security measures; missing headers are also an OWASP baseline failure.
Privacy policy reachable from homepageGDPR Art. 13: information must be provided before data collection. An unreachable policy undermines the whole legal basis.
Forms with privacy notice linksEvery data-collecting form needs a visible link to your privacy policy.
Fair warning: a passing score today is not permanent. Marketing adds scripts, CMP settings drift, tag managers change. Pro ($79/year) re-scans your site daily and alerts you when compliance breaks.

Why "free" doesn't mean shallow

Most free GDPR checkers test one thing — whether you have a cookie banner. That misses what regulators actually fine for. This scanner looks at how your site behaves:

  1. Pre-consent tracking. It detects known analytics and ad scripts (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok, Hotjar and more) in the served HTML, alongside whether a consent platform is present to gate them.
  2. Consent plumbing. A banner that exists but isn't wired to Google Consent Mode v2 or IAB TCF means choices may never reach your ad stack — the exact gap behind recent multi-million-euro decisions.
  3. Security baseline. TLS validity plus CSP, HSTS and related headers — GDPR Art. 32's "appropriate technical measures" in their most measurable form.

Frequently asked questions

Is this GDPR scanner really free?

Yes. Scanning any URL is free, unlimited, and requires no account or credit card. The paid product, Pro ($79/year), adds daily re-scans, email alerts and auditor-ready PDF documentation — you only need it if you must prove compliance over time.

Does it work outside WordPress?

Yes — that is the point. The scanner analyses HTTP responses and HTML markup, so it works identically on Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Next.js, Drupal, Joomla, static HTML and custom stacks. No plugin, app or server access needed.

Do you store my URL or scan results?

No. Each scan runs in memory and returns its result immediately. No URLs, IPs or scan data are logged or stored.

Is the result a legal opinion?

No. The scanner identifies technical patterns — missing consent signals, trackers without gating, absent security headers. These are strong indicators of compliance risk, but only a qualified lawyer can give legal advice about your specific situation.

What should I do if my site fails?

Start with the failing checks in the report: each one names the concrete pattern found. For consent issues, review your CMP configuration so scripts only load after consent, and connect Google Consent Mode v2. Re-scan after each fix — it takes ten seconds.
