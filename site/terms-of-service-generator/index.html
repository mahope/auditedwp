The terms of service generator has been taken down.

It produced documents that looked legal without a lawyer behind them. I am not comfortable offering that, so it is gone.

What you can do instead: the compliance checklist lists what a document like this must contain, and the free EU Website Compliance Guide 2026 explains each point in plain language. With those in hand, a lawyer or a reputable template service will take you the rest of the way.

If you are checking whether your site links to the documents it needs, run the scan. That part still works and always will.
