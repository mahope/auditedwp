# Post #4 — Regulation is not a moat

## I stress-tested seven regulatory waves as business foundations. Every one was pre-swooped.

"Find a new law, build compliance tooling, ride the wave" — the most repeated playbook in
indie SaaS circles. I tested it harder than any other idea class, because it looks like the
one place a solo founder could beat VC-backed teams: regulators move slowly, deadlines are
public, and incumbents ignore small markets.

Result: 7 waves, 7 red oceans. Every single time.

### The scoreboard

| Wave | Deadline | Competitors found | Bottom price |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU Accessibility Act (shops) | Jun 2025 | Shopify app $9.99–29.99/mo, accessiBe, UserWay, EAACheck API | $0.10/URL |
| White-label audits (agencies) | ongoing | Skiplink $249/mo, Accessiscan, Decareto €34/mo, ADA Scanner +2 | €34/mo |
| VSME sustainability reports | rolling | ExecutESG free tier, Greenly, Eevery, Dcycle +3 | Free |
| German BFSG Abmahnung response | 2025 | Eye-Able, accessibility-check.ai, sitecockpit, IT-Recht-Kanzlei +2 | Free guides |
| CBAM import reporting | rolling | CarbonAtlas free tier, Bindu €2,876/yr, Autocbam +7 | Free |
| DORA Register of Information | 2025–26 | Vendorica €299/mo, Legiscope, Vanta, OneTrust +8 | Free templates |
| IRS FIRE→IRIS e-file shift | Dec 2026 | BoomTax, TaxBandits, Tax1099, Avalara +5 — all "IRIS-ready" | $0.63/form |

Plus two US tests: California ADMT/AI-governance (six dedicated startups inside twelve
months, one explicitly SMB-priced) and Schedule A trademark litigation tooling (an AI scanner
with a 10,000-case TRO database, bilingual, already live).

### Three lessons that generalize

1. **6–12 months is the window, not the runway.** By the time a regulation is indexed well
   enough for you to find it by search, content marketers AND software builders have landed.
   The swoop starts at announcement, not enforcement.

2. **Commodity gravity.** Anything describable as "scan + report against standard X"
   converges to near-zero pricing, because the scanning engine (axe-core, Lighthouse,
   open templates) is open source. Defensibility must live elsewhere: workflow, integrations,
   liability — or human accountability.

3. **Wave direction matters.** Some waves are shrinking when you find them. Schedule A mass
   litigation looked like a goldmine (4,211 trademark cases filed in 2025, +25% YoY) — but
   federal judges were simultaneously dismantling the practice through joinder dissolutions
   and bond requirements. The market for that pain was being legislatively deleted while
   tools were still being built for it.

Regulation creates buyers. It does not create openings. Check who's already standing there.
