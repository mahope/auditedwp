Six compliance checks, one click

Click "Run Scan" in your WordPress admin. EUComply checks your site against the six most common EU compliance requirements — and tells you exactly what to fix.

🔒 SSL & HTTPS

Checks if your site runs on HTTPS with HSTS headers. Without it, GDPR Art. 32 (security) and browser "Not Secure" warnings hurt trust.

🍪 Cookie Consent

Detects GDPR cookie consent plugins (Complianz, Cookiebot, CookieYes, WP Consent API). Flags sites missing consent mechanisms — ePrivacy Directive requirement.

📋 GDPR Forms

Scans for form plugins (CF7, WPForms, Gravity Forms) and checks if a Privacy Policy page is linked. Missing consent checkboxes on forms = high GDPR risk.

💾 Backup Status

Verifies backup plugins are active and reports last backup age. NIS2 Art. 18 requires recovery capability — no backup = no recovery.

🔧 Plugin & Core Health

Checks for outdated WordPress core and plugins. Each outdated plugin is a potential CVE entry — DORA Art. 6 requires vulnerability management.

📄 Legal Pages

Looks for Privacy Policy, Imprint/Impressum, and accessibility statement pages. EAA requires an accessibility statement; GDPR requires a privacy policy.

FAQ

Does the plugin send my data anywhere?

No. All checks run locally on your WordPress server. No data is sent to external APIs, no user tracking, no telemetry. The Pro PDF report is generated server-side and delivered as a download.

Which regulations does it cover?

EUComply checks against requirements from: GDPR (Art. 5, 25, 32 — data protection, privacy by design, security), NIS2 (Art. 18 — recovery, Art. 21 — supply chain), DORA (Art. 6 — ICT risk, Art. 28 — third-party), and EAA (accessibility statement requirement). The checks are not legal advice — they flag technical gaps for further review.

Who needs this?

WordPress site owners in the EU (or serving EU customers) who need to document compliance. Typical users: web agencies managing client sites, e-commerce operators, SaaS companies, and any business subject to EU procurement or insurance compliance reviews.

Can I use the Pro report for audits?

Yes — the Pro PDF report is designed to be auditor-ready. It documents the scan date, methodology, and findings. For formal compliance certification, consult a qualified auditor — EUComply gives you the technical baseline.

Will this slow down my site?

No. The scan runs on demand or via WordPress cron (weekly). No page-load impact. The plugin adds ~0.01 KB of CSS to admin pages only — nothing on the frontend.

What about the European Accessibility Act (EAA)?

The EAA (effective June 2025) requires EU websites to be accessible. EUComply checks for an accessibility statement page and helps you generate one with Pro. Full WCAG audit requires a dedicated tool — the plugin tells you where to start.
