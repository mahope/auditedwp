FAQ

Are you really fully white-label?

Yes. We work under NDA and never contact your clients. Reports, dashboards and communication carry your branding; templates are provided at onboarding.

What exactly does the "audit trail" contain?

Per site: every plugin/core/theme update applied (before/after versions), every backup taken and tested, every security patch, every restore — timestamped with operator identity. Exportable as PDF/CSV for audits, insurers or client reviews. See a worked example →

Where is data hosted?

All operational data and backups reside within the EU under EU jurisdiction. A standard DPA is signed during onboarding.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency response?

No — deliberately. We offer next-business-day recovery and scheduled maintenance windows with a documented process. If your clients require 24/7 incident response, we're not the right partner, and we'd rather say that upfront.

Which platforms do you cover?

WordPress (single sites and multisite), including WooCommerce. Not a WordPress shop? Ask — we take a limited number of non-WP CMS engagements.

Can we start with just one site to test you?

Yes — the Starter tier exists precisely for that. Most agencies pilot with one high-visibility client site before moving their whole portfolio.
