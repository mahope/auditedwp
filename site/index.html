FAQ

Does this work on my platform?

Yes — the scanner checks HTTP responses and HTML markup. It works on any website: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, Next.js, Drupal, Joomla, static HTML, or custom stacks. The only requirement is a public URL.

Do I need to install something?

No. The online scanner works entirely from your browser — just enter a URL. If you use WordPress, you can optionally install the free plugin to run scans from your admin dashboard with additional WordPress-specific checks (backup status, plugin health, core version).

Which regulations does it cover?

EUComply checks against requirements from: GDPR (Art. 5, 13, 25, 32 — data protection, privacy by design, security), NIS2 (Art. 18 — recovery, Art. 21 — supply chain), DORA (Art. 6 — ICT risk), and EAA (accessibility statement requirement). The checks identify technical gaps — they are not legal advice.

Is my data stored or tracked?

No. The scanner fetches the URL, runs checks in memory, and returns results. No scan data, URLs, or IPs are logged or stored. The WordPress plugin runs everything on your own server — no data leaves your installation.

Who needs Pro?

Anyone who needs documented proof of compliance — web agencies managing client sites, SaaS companies responding to vendor questionnaires, businesses subject to EU procurement or insurance reviews. Pro generates auditor-ready PDFs and legal templates you can forward directly.

What about the European Accessibility Act (EAA)?

The EAA (effective June 2025) requires accessible digital services. EUComply checks for an accessibility statement page on your site. Pro generates an EAA conformance statement template. Full WCAG audit requires a dedicated tool — the scanner tells you where to start.
