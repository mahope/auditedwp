How can it be one-time?

Because there are no servers between your machine and your sites.

Your machine does the checking

Cloud monitors pay for data centers 24/7 and bill you monthly to cover it. DeskUptime runs those same HTTP, TLS and content checks locally — zero infrastructure cost after purchase.

No account, no data trail

Nothing to sign up for, nothing phoning home. Your URL list lives on your disk, not in someone else's database. That privacy is a feature, not a cost saving we apologize for.

Free tier that isn't crippled

The CLI (npx github:mahope/deskuptime check <url>) is free forever and open source. You can verify exactly how checks work before spending anything.

FAQ

Is there really an uptime monitor with no monthly fee?

Yes — DeskUptime is a desktop app. You pay $19 once for a license key and it keeps checking your sites from your machine. Free routes also exist (cron scripts, self-hosted Uptime Kuma) but they mean building or hosting something yourself.

What's the catch compared to Pingdom or UptimeRobot?

The catch is honest and stated above: checks stop when your computer is off, and there are no team dashboards or hosted status pages. Cloud SaaS earns its subscription for teams that need those. Solo developers and freelancers usually don't.

Do I need to keep my laptop awake?

For continuous watch mode, yes. Quick manual checks work any time the machine is on. Many users pair DeskUptime during the day with a free cloud tier overnight — and still save money versus a paid SaaS plan.

Does it work with any website or CMS?

Yes. Checks happen at the HTTP/TLS level, so WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, static sites and API endpoints all behave identically. Nothing gets installed on your server.

What happens after I pay the $19?

You receive a license key by email instantly via Lemon Squeezy. Enter it once in the desktop app and Pro features get — 3 machines per license. 14-day refund policy, no questions asked.
