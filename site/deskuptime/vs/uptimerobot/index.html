DeskUptime vs UptimeRobot

Both monitor uptime, SSL, and response times. One costs $7/month forever. The other costs $19 once. Guess which is which.

Winner if you want to save money:

DeskUptime — $19

vs $420 over 5 years for UptimeRobot Pro. Same core functionality.

When would you pick UptimeRobot?

UptimeRobot wins on convenience. It runs in the cloud — you don't need your computer on. Their free tier (50 checks/5 min) is generous, and their paid Pro plans add SMS alerts, public status pages, and faster check intervals.

Choose UptimeRobot if: you need 24/7 cloud-based monitoring with no local machine, or you rely on their public status pages for customers.

When would you pick DeskUptime?

DeskUptime wins on price and privacy. One payment covers you for life. Your URLs never touch a third-party server. The desktop app runs in the system tray and alerts you natively — no email noise.

Choose DeskUptime if: you're a freelancer or small team monitoring your own sites, you already have your computer on during work hours, and you'd rather pay $19 once than $84 every year.

Can you use both?

Yes, many do. UptimeRobot's free tier for cloud coverage while you're asleep + DeskUptime for detailed checks and content monitoring during the day. The CLI gives you instant on-demand checks without opening a browser.

Why DeskUptime wins

  • $19 vs $84+/year — breakeven in 3 months
  • Privacy: no third-party server sees your URLs
  • Content change detection not in UptimeRobot
  • CLI for instant checks from terminal
  • Unlimited monitors — no tier caps

What UptimeRobot does better

  • Cloud-based: works without your computer on
  • Public status pages for customers
  • Free tier is genuinely useful
  • SMS, push notification integrations
