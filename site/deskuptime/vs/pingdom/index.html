When Pingdom still makes sense

Pingdom is a mature enterprise-grade platform. If you need uptime checks from 7+ global locations, public status pages, or team collaboration features, Pingdom's $15/month is a legitimate business expense. Their multi-location checks catch region-specific outages.

When DeskUptime wins

For freelancers, developers, and small agencies managing their own sites, Pingdom is expensive overkill. DeskUptime covers uptime, SSL, and content checks at a fraction of the cost. Save $161 in year one.
