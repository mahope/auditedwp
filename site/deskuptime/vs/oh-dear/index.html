When Oh Dear is the right call

Honestly: Oh Dear is a great product, built by well-known developers in the Laravel community. It checks every minute from multiple global locations, crawls every page for broken links and mixed content, watches SSL chains, DNS, cron jobs and Lighthouse performance — all included on every plan, with status pages and white-label client reporting. If you run an agency responsible for dozens of client sites and need to prove health to customers, that bundle is worth paying for.

When DeskUptime makes more sense

If you're monitoring your own projects — freelance portfolio, client sites you built, side projects — the question is simpler: "are my sites up, is the certificate valid, did something change?" DeskUptime answers exactly that from the computer you already work on, for $19 total instead of a recurring bill that grows with each site you add.

Why DeskUptime wins

  • $19 once vs €15+/month — breakeven in ~5 weeks
  • Unlimited URLs vs per-site pricing tiers
  • No cloud account; your URLs stay on your machine
  • CLI + Desktop dual interface
  • Nothing renews, nothing to cancel

What Oh Dear does better

  • Checks run even when your computer sleeps
  • Global multi-location checks every minute
  • Broken-link crawler and mixed-content detection
  • DNS and Lighthouse performance monitoring
  • Status pages and white-label client reports
