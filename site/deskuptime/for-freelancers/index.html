FAQ

How is this different from a SaaS uptime monitor?

DeskUptime runs on your computer, not on a cloud server. That means no monthly subscription, no per-URL pricing, and your check data never leaves your machine. The trade-off: for 24/7 coverage your computer needs to be on. Most freelancers work on their laptop during business hours — that's when monitoring matters most.

Can I monitor client sites without a monthly fee?

Yes. That's exactly what DeskUptime is built for. One $19 purchase covers unlimited URLs on up to 3 machines. No upgrade tiers, no per-client pricing, no surprise when you add a new site.

Does it work on any type of website?

Any website that responds to HTTPS — WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Next.js, Squarespace, static HTML, or a custom app. DeskUptime just checks URLs. It doesn't care what framework or CMS you use.

What happens if my computer is asleep?

No checks run while the computer is off. When you wake it up, DeskUptime resumes its schedule. For after-hours coverage you can run it on a cheap always-on device (Raspberry Pi, old laptop) — same $19 license covers all your machines.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. Install the desktop app or CLI — the free tier lets you run on-demand checks for any URL. Upgrade to Pro when you need background monitoring and notifications.
