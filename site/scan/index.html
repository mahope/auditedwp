What the scan checks

Nine automated checks based on EU requirements that apply to every website serving EU users — regardless of platform.

🔒 HTTPS + HSTS

TLS and Strict-Transport-Security. GDPR Art. 32 requires appropriate technical security measures.

🍪 Cookie consent

Detects 15+ consent platforms in served HTML (Cookiebot, OneTrust, CookieYes…). ePrivacy Directive requires prior consent.

🔔 Google Consent Mode v2

Detects Consent Mode v2 signals (gtag, dataLayer, ad_storage). Required by Google since March 2024 for EU ad personalization — without it, conversion tracking is restricted.

📋 Forms & privacy link

Forms found without a visible privacy-policy link are a top GDPR enforcement target (Art. 13).

📄 Legal pages

Privacy policy, imprint, terms, and EAA accessibility statement linked from your homepage.

🛡️ Security headers

CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, frame protection — OWASP baseline, NIS2 Art. 21.

🔍 Platform fingerprint

Identifies your CMS/stack so advice is relevant. Purely informational — nothing is stored.

🏦 DORA resilience

DORA (EU 2022/2554) effective Jan 2025. Checks for incident reporting, business continuity, ICT risk management and resilience testing disclosures on financial/ICT entity sites.

📢 IAB TCF

Detects IAB Transparency & Consent Framework — used by ad-tech platforms and publishers for GDPR-compliant programmatic advertising.

🎯 Trackers without consent

Detects Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok, Hotjar and 8 more trackers loaded without a consent platform. Loading non-essential trackers before consent breaches GDPR Art. 6 and the ePrivacy Directive. 9 checks total.
