Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Summary

DevNotify is a local application. We do not collect, store or transmit your personal data. There is no account system and no telemetry.

Website visits and downloads

This website counts anonymous, aggregate page visits and download clicks. The counter stores no cookies, no identifiers and no IP addresses on our side — each visit is counted at most once per hour, then discarded. No individual browsing behaviour is recorded or profiled.

Your GitHub token

The GitHub personal access token you provide is stored only on your Mac (in the app's local settings) and is sent only to api.github.com to fetch your notifications. We never see it.

Launch notification list

If you ask us to notify you when checkout opens, we store only the email address you submit, until launch. It is used for exactly one message and then deleted. No cookies, no tracking, never shared with third parties. You can have it removed any time by writing to the address below.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record). When you buy a license, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment details and email address under their privacy policy. We receive only what is needed to issue your license key.
