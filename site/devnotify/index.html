FAQ

Does my token leave my computer?

No. It is stored locally by the app and used only to call api.github.com directly from your machine.

"App can't be opened" on macOS first launch

The DMG isn't signed with an Apple Developer certificate yet. Right-click DevNotify.app → Open → Open. You only have to do this once.

What does the trial include?

Everything. Full functionality for 7 days on every platform. After that, a $19 lifetime license unlocks it permanently.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS 11 Big Sur and later (Apple Silicon M1–M4 and Intel), Windows 10 and 11, and Linux (.deb packages and AppImage). See the download page with SHA-256 checksums for every file.

Is one license valid on all my computers?

Yes. Buy once, use DevNotify on your Mac, your Windows PC and your Linux box.

Lifetime — really?

Yes. One payment, perpetual use of version 1.x including updates.
