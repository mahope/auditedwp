## About this template

The European Accessibility Act (EAA) entered into force June 28, 2025. From June 28, 2025 onward, products and services that fall under the directive must publish an accessibility statement that meets EN 301 549 standards and the implementing national laws of each EU member state.

This template gives you a compliant starting point. Fill in the bracketed placeholders, adjust the conformance status to match your actual level, and publish the statement on your website or with your product.

## Instructions

1. **Fill in brackets** — replace all `[bracketed text]` with your actual details.
2. **Check conformance** — be honest about your current level. "Partially conformant" with a clear remediation plan is better than claiming full conformance you haven't verified.
3. **Publish** — place the statement at `/accessibility` or `/accessibility-statement` on your website. Link to it from the site footer.
4. **Review annually** — EAA enforcement requires the statement to stay current.
