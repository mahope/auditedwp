# WHITE-LABEL MUTUAL NDA — CLAUSE SET
AuditedWP template v1.0 — 2026-08-23. Review by counsel before first use.

Core clauses (attach to agency agreement or sign standalone):

1. **Definition.** "Confidential Information" = all non-public information
   exchanged by the parties, including client lists, site inventories,
   credentials, pricing, and the existence and terms of this engagement.

2. **No-client-contact (the key white-label clause).** Processor shall not
   contact, market to, solicit, or accept direct work from any of Agency's
   clients identified during the engagement, during the term and for
   24 months after termination. Processor communicates with client sites
   only through technical access granted by Agency.

3. **Anonymity.** Processor operates under Agency's branding in all
   client-facing outputs. Processor shall not disclose its role to Agency's
   clients except where legally compelled (with prior notice to Agency).

4. **Use limitation.** Confidential Information used solely to deliver the
   maintenance services. No aggregation, publication, or benchmarking use.

5. **Return/destruction.** On termination, all Confidential Information
   returned or destroyed within 30 days; written confirmation provided.
   Retained legal records exempted, listed in writing.

6. **Term.** Obligations survive 24 months from last disclosure.

7. **Governing law.** [Danish law / other], exclusive jurisdiction [city].
