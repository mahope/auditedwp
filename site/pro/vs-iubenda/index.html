EUComply vs Iubenda: which one fits EU compliance work?

Updated 29 August 2026 · Comparison GDPR EAA

Iubenda is the most prominent Italy-born compliance suite for websites — best known for its privacy and cookie policy generator and its consent management product. If you are evaluating it against alternatives, this page compares it with EUComply Pro honestly, including what both tools do not do. Pricing details change often, so always confirm current numbers on the vendor's own site.

The short version: Iubenda is strong on documents and consent — policies, banners and preference management across many jurisdictions. EUComply is an automated monitoring tool focused on the technical side of European obligations — GDPR tracker issues, SSL hardening, German Impressum, NIS2 signals, the European Accessibility Act and DORA vendor requirements — with daily scans, PDF reports you can hand to a client or auditor, and a flat $79/year price.

What Iubenda does better

  • Serving consent. A certified consent-management platform with granular preference centres, cross-domain sync and ad-tech integrations (IAB TCF, Google Consent Mode). We deliberately don't build banners.
  • Document generation. A deep, continuously maintained library of jurisdiction-aware policy documents — broader than our template set.
  • Ecosystem depth. Native plugins and integrations for many platforms, plus legal-design styling for banners and documents.

What EUComply does better

  • The technical half of compliance. Iubenda's focus is consent and paperwork. Our checks target obligations that document tools never verify: the German Impressum requirement, NIS2 exposure signals, EAA accessibility basics, DORA vendor documentation and server-side security headers like HSTS.
  • Monitoring instead of one-off setup. A policy generated once says nothing about your site next week. EUComply Pro rescans daily, keeps 30 days of history and emails you when a check starts failing — so a broken update doesn't silently take you out of compliance.
  • Evidence you can hand over. Timestamped PDF reports suitable for clients, auditors or management are included in every plan — not locked behind a top tier.
  • Predictable price. One flat $79/year per site covering everything we do. No per-product subscriptions, no feature gates that move as your traffic grows.

Which should you pick?

  • You need a cookie banner and generated legal policies: Iubenda (or another dedicated CMP) is the right category of tool.
  • You sell into the EU and want to know — every day — that your site still meets the technical and documentary requirements: that's exactly what EUComply Pro does. The two complement each other well: a CMP for consent, EUComply for monitoring everything else.
  • You're an agency managing client sites in the EU: daily monitoring plus branded PDF evidence per site is usually the missing piece either way.
Honest limits: no automated scanner can prove legal compliance — only reduce risk and surface issues. Neither tool replaces legal advice. And if your primary need is serving user consent itself, neither our scanner nor any monitor substitutes for a real consent-management platform.
