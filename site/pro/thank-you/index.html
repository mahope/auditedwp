You're in. Welcome to EUComply Pro.

Your subscription is active. Here is exactly what happens next — it takes about 2 minutes to get set up.

  1. 1

    Check your email

    We sent your account credentials and a link to the Pro dashboard to the email you used during checkout. If you don't see it within 2 minutes, check your spam folder.

  2. 2

    Add your first website

    Log into the dashboard and enter the URL of the site you want to monitor. You can add multiple sites — each has its own subscription.

  3. 3

    Get your compliance badge

    Copy the embed snippet from the dashboard and paste it into your website footer. Visitors see your live score — and can click to verify it.

  4. 4

    Download your templates

    Grab the DPA template, NIS2 vendor clause set, and EAA accessibility statement from the dashboard. Fill in your details — they're ready to use.

Your first scan is queued

The automated daily scan starts running today. Your first auditor-ready PDF report will be available within 24 hours. You'll also receive an email with the initial results.

Open the dashboard →

Need help?

Start with the free scanner for instant checks. Visit the guides section for step-by-step GDPR, NIS2, and EAA walkthroughs. For anything else, use the contact form — we reply within 24 hours.

Your data stays yours. No lock-in. Cancel anytime through the dashboard.
