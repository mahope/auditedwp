EUComply Pro

The free scanner tells you what is wrong today. Pro will keep checking, tell you when something breaks, and give you the paperwork to show it was fixed.

Pro launches when checkout is live; the free scanner is complete.

What Pro will include

  • Daily re-scans

    The nine checks, run every day, with a 30-day history so you can see when a header disappeared or a tracker crept in.

  • Email on a failing check

    One message when a check goes from pass to fail. Nothing when everything is fine.

  • PDF report

    Findings and dates in a document you can hand to a client, an auditor or an insurer.

  • Footer badge

    A small badge that links to the latest scan result, so visitors can see for themselves.

  • Document templates

    Data processing agreement, NIS2 and DORA vendor clauses and an accessibility statement, included.

79 USD per website, per year. The full comparison of Free, Pro and the templates is on the pricing page. There is no waiting list, and there is no buy button until payment works.
