Frequently asked questions

Quick answers — if something's missing, use the free scanner right now.

Do I need to run WordPress?

No. EUComply Pro works on every website — WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Next.js, Squarespace, Wix, Drupal, static HTML, or custom stacks. The only requirement is a public URL.

How does the daily scan work?

You register your URL once. Our server performs a full compliance check once per day and stores the results. You get a dashboard with your score history, PDF reports, and email alerts on score drops.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access to your existing reports and downloads. Daily scanning and alerts stop at the end of your billing period. No lock-in, no data hostage.

Are the templates actual legal documents?

They are professional templates with guidance comments — written for agencies and consultants, not law firms. They cover the clauses most EU compliance audits check. For high-risk contracts, have counsel review the filled-in result (minutes, not hours).

Can I try before I buy?

Yes — use the free scanner right now. It runs the same checks. Pro adds daily monitoring, PDF reports, the badge, and templates. Try the scanner first; if you need the paperwork, Pro is ready.

Can I add multiple sites?

Pro is $79 per site per year. Multiple sites each need their own subscription — each site has its own scan schedule, dashboard and badge. There is no bulk discount yet, but it's on the roadmap.
