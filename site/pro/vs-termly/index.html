EUComply vs Termly: which one fits EU compliance work?

Termly is one of the best-known names in website privacy compliance — especially for generating cookie banners and policy documents. If you are evaluating it against alternatives, this page compares it with EUComply Pro honestly, including what both tools do not do. Pricing details change often, so always confirm current numbers on the vendor's own site.

The short version: Termly is strong on consent management and policy generation, primarily from a US perspective. EUComply is an automated monitoring tool focused specifically on European obligations — GDPR tracker issues, SSL hardening, German Impressum, NIS2 signals, the European Accessibility Act and DORA vendor requirements — with daily scans, PDF reports you can hand to a client or auditor, and a flat $79/year price.

What Termly does better

  • Serving consent. If you need a fully managed cookie-consent platform with granular preference centres and IAB TCF certification, Termly's consent product is more complete than anything we offer. We deliberately don't build banners.
  • Document generation at scale. A large library of jurisdiction-aware policy generators, maintained continuously as laws change.
  • Maturity. A large customer base and brand recognition — relevant if procurement requires a known vendor name.

What EUComply does better

  • European scope, not just GDPR paperwork. Termly's focus is consent and policies. Our checks target obligations that most consent tools never touch: the German Impressum requirement, NIS2 exposure, the European Accessibility Act, DORA vendor documentation and server-side security headers like HSTS.
  • Monitoring instead of one-off setup. Compliance is not a checkbox you tick once. EUComply Pro rescans your site daily, keeps 30 days of history and emails you when a check starts failing — so a broken update doesn't silently take you out of compliance.
  • Evidence you can hand over. Every plan includes timestamped PDF reports suitable for clients, auditors or management — not locked behind a top tier.
  • Predictable price. One flat $79/year per site. No feature gates, no per-impression consent billing, no surprise tier upgrades when your traffic grows.

Which should you pick?

  • You mainly need a cookie banner and generated policies: Termly (or a dedicated CMP) is the right category of tool.
  • You sell into the EU and want to know — every day — that your site still meets the technical and documentary requirements: that's exactly what EUComply Pro does. Many businesses use both: a CMP for consent, EUComply for monitoring everything else.
  • You're an agency managing client sites in the EU: daily monitoring plus branded PDF evidence per site is usually the missing piece either way.
Honest limits: no automated scanner can prove legal compliance — only reduce risk and surface issues. Neither tool replaces legal advice. And if your primary need is serving user consent itself, neither our scanner nor any monitor substitutes for a real consent-management platform.
