EUComply vs Cookiebot: which one fits EU compliance work?

Updated 29 August 2026 · Comparison GDPR NIS2

Cookiebot (by Usercentrics) is probably the most recognised consent management platform in Europe — the tool many sites use to collect and document cookie consent. If you are evaluating it against alternatives, this page compares it with EUComply Pro honestly, including what both tools do not do. Pricing details change often, so always confirm current numbers on the vendor's own site.

The short version: Cookiebot is strong on consent collection and cookie documentation — it serves the banner and logs user choices. EUComply is an automated monitoring tool focused on everything a CMP doesn't cover: SSL hardening, security headers, German Impressum, privacy-policy and accessibility-statement links, NIS2 exposure signals, European Accessibility Act basics and DORA vendor documentation — with daily scans, PDF reports you can hand to a client or auditor, and a flat $79/year price.

What Cookiebot does better

  • Serving consent itself. A certified CMP with granular preference centres, consent logging that stands up in an audit of your cookie practices, and IAB TCF support. We deliberately don't build banners — if you need one, a real CMP is the right category of tool.
  • Deep cookie inventory. A maintained database categorising thousands of cookies and their owners.
  • Maturity. A very large installed base across Europe — relevant if procurement requires a known vendor name.

What EUComply does better

  • Everything outside the consent box. A CMP answers "do you collect consent properly?" It says nothing about whether your SSL/HSTS configuration is sane, whether your security headers are set, whether an Impressum exists for German visitors, or whether you can evidence NIS2/EAA/DORA awareness. Those are our nine checks.
  • Monitoring instead of one-off setup. Consent is collected once per visitor; compliance drifts daily. EUComply Pro rescans your site every day, keeps 30 days of history and emails you when a check starts failing.
  • Evidence you can hand over. Every plan includes timestamped PDF reports suitable for clients, auditors or management — not locked behind a top tier.
  • Predictable price. One flat $79/year per site. No traffic-based tier jumps — a busy site costs the same as a quiet one.

Which should you pick?

  • You need to collect and document cookie consent: Cookiebot is exactly built for that, and good at it.
  • You already run a CMP but have no idea whether the rest of your site still meets EU technical requirements: that gap is precisely what EUComply Pro fills. Many sites sensibly run both.
  • You're an agency managing client sites in the EU: daily monitoring plus branded PDF evidence per site is usually the missing piece either way.
Honest limits: no automated scanner can prove legal compliance — only reduce risk and surface issues. Neither tool replaces legal advice. And if your primary need is serving user consent itself, neither our scanner nor any monitor substitutes for a real consent-management platform.
