EUComply vs OneTrust: which one fits EU compliance work?

Updated 29 August 2026 · Comparison GDPR NIS2

OneTrust is the largest privacy-management platform on the market — consent management, cookie scanning, data-subject-request workflows, assessments and third-party-risk tooling in one enterprise suite, aimed primarily at mid-market and enterprise privacy teams. If you are evaluating it against alternatives, this page compares it with EUComply Pro honestly, including what both tools do not do. Pricing details change often and are typically quote-based annual contracts, so always confirm current numbers with the vendor.

The short version: OneTrust is strong on consent collection, privacy-program management and vendor risk — it serves the banner, runs your assessments and tracks data-subject requests at enterprise scale. EUComply is an automated monitoring tool focused on everything a CMP suite doesn't cover on your actual live site: SSL hardening, security headers, German Impressum, privacy-policy and accessibility-statement links, NIS2 exposure signals, European Accessibility Act basics and DORA vendor documentation — with daily scans, PDF reports you can hand to a client or auditor, and a flat $79/year price.

What OneTrust does better

  • Serving and documenting consent at scale. A mature CMP with granular preference controls, consent logging across hundreds of sites and deep regulatory coverage. We deliberately don't build banners — if you need one, a real CMP is the right category of tool.
  • Breadth of the privacy program. Assessments, data mapping, DSAR automation, incident response and ESG modules in one platform — built for dedicated privacy teams with budgets to match.
  • Enterprise integration. SSO, audit trails and procurement-grade controls that large organisations require.

What EUComply does better

  • Everything outside the consent box. An enterprise suite answers "do you collect consent and manage privacy properly?" It says nothing about whether your SSL/HSTS configuration is sane, whether your security headers are set, whether an Impressum exists for German visitors, or whether you can evidence NIS2/EAA/DORA awareness. Those are our nine checks.
  • A price a small business can say yes to. If you run one to ten sites, an enterprise quote process is the wrong tool for the job. Flat $79/year per site, self-serve checkout, no sales call.
  • Monitoring instead of one-off setup. Compliance drifts daily. EUComply Pro rescans your site every day, keeps 30 days of history and emails you when a check starts failing.
  • Evidence you can hand over. Every plan includes timestamped PDF reports suitable for clients, auditors or management — not locked behind a top tier.

Which should you pick?

  • You're an enterprise privacy team running assessments, DSARs and multi-site consent: OneTrust is built for exactly that, and good at it.
  • You already run a CMP but have no idea whether the rest of your site still meets EU technical requirements: that gap is precisely what EUComply Pro fills. Many sites sensibly run both.
  • You're an agency or small business managing client sites in the EU: daily monitoring plus auditor-ready PDF evidence per site at $79/year is usually the missing piece either way — without an enterprise procurement cycle.
Honest limits: no automated scanner can prove legal compliance — only reduce risk and surface issues. Neither tool replaces legal advice. And if your primary need is serving user consent or running a full privacy program, neither our scanner nor any monitor substitutes for a privacy-management platform.
