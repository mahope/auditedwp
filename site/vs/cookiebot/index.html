Verdict

Choose EUComply if:

You want a free, universal compliance scanner that works on every platform. Or you need affordable daily monitoring at $79/year with no domain limits, no page caps, and PDF audit reports. EUComply covers more regulations (GDPR, NIS2, DORA, EAA) than Cookiebot's cookie-focused CMP.

Keep Cookiebot if:

You need a full cookie consent management platform (CMP) with a banner, consent database, and IAB TCF support. Cookiebot is a CMP first; EUComply is a compliance scanner first. The best setup for many sites is Cookiebot (or another CMP) for consent + EUComply for compliance scanning.
