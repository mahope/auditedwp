Verdict

Choose EUComply if:

You need a free, comprehensive compliance scanner that covers NIS2, DORA, and EAA in addition to GDPR. Or you want affordable daily monitoring with PDF audit reports at $79/year with no domain limits.

Choose iubenda if:

You need lawyer-vetted privacy policies, Terms of Service, and cookie solutions with 1,600+ pre-built clauses. iubenda's legal documents are industry standard — their scanner is complementary.

Best of both:

Use iubenda for your legal documents and cookie banner. Use EUComply (free) for weekly compliance scans and Pro for daily monitoring. Together they cover the full compliance lifecycle: documents + monitoring.
