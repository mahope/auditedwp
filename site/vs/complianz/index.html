EUComply vs Complianz (2026): Every Platform, Not Just WordPress

Complianz is a well-loved privacy suite with over a million installs — but it only works inside WordPress, and its licenses are priced per site. EUComply scans any website on any platform and monitors unlimited domains from $79/year.

The WordPress boundary: Complianz's Privacy Suite requires a WordPress installation. A Shopify store, a Webflow site, a Next.js app or a hand-coded landing page simply cannot run it — and each additional WordPress site needs a bigger license.

The Honest Trade-off

We think you deserve the counter-argument stated plainly.

If your entire web presence is one WordPress site and you need a consent banner plus auto-generated cookie and privacy policies, Complianz at €59/year is a genuinely good deal. It generates documents we don't, and its banner ships with more regions than we cover. — Our own assessment

Where EUComply wins is everywhere outside that single scenario: non-WordPress platforms, multiple sites, agencies, scheduled re-scanning, NIS2/DORA/EAA coverage, and an API you can build on.

Verdict

Choose EUComply if:

Your sites live on any platform other than (or alongside) WordPress, you manage more than a couple of domains, or you want continuous monitoring with alerts instead of a point-in-time plugin. Free to find out — paste any URL and get results in seconds.

Keep Complianz if:

You have exactly one WordPress site and want banner + generated legal documents in one plugin. The best setup for many WordPress owners is Complianz (or another CMP) for consent + EUComply for independent, external compliance scanning.
