Verdict

Choose EUComply if:

You want to know where your site stands against GDPR, NIS2, DORA and EAA requirements today, for free — and you want flat-rate daily monitoring at $79/year without session tiers, auto-upgrades, or a configuration project. Works identically whether your site runs on WordPress, Shopify, Next.js or plain HTML.

Keep Usercentrics if:

You run a publisher-grade ad stack that needs IAB TCF vendor management, app/CTV consent SDKs, or server-side tracking. That is genuinely what Usercentrics is built for. The best setup for many sites is Usercentrics (or another CMP) for consent + EUComply for compliance scanning.
