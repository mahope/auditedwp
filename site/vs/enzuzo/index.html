EUComply vs Enzuzo (2026): Scanner vs Privacy Management Platform

Enzuzo bundles policy generators, DSAR workflows, and cookie consent into a privacy suite. EUComply is a universal compliance scanner that audits any live site for GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and EAA gaps — no script required.

Verdict

Choose EUComply if:

You want a free, independent compliance audit of any website — including NIS2, DORA, and EAA checks that privacy suites don't perform. Or you want daily monitoring with PDF audit reports at $79/year across unlimited domains.

Choose Enzuzo if:

You need privacy policy generation, cookie consent, and DSAR request handling managed directly on your site as one integrated workflow.

Best of both:

Run Enzuzo for policies and consent. Use EUComply (free) to independently verify your site passes — and Pro for daily external monitoring that catches regressions before customers or regulators do.
