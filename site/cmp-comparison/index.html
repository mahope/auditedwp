Cookiebot by Usercentrics (acquired 2021)

Best for: small & mid-size sites • Auto-scanning • Google-certified

Free – €12/mo for most sites

Cookiebot automatically discovers and categorizes all cookies on your site, then serves a compliant banner. Free for sites under 100 pages.

✅ Pros: Best-in-class auto-scanning • Google Consent Mode v2 certified • IAB TCF 2.2 • 20+ languages • Free tier for small sites • 30% recurring affiliate commission
❌ Cons: Premium pricing at scale (€28+/mo for larger sites) • Banner customization limited on lower tiers
Verdict: The safest choice if you want the industry standard. Used by 150,000+ websites. The free tier makes it trivially easy to try.

Complianz by Privacy Suite

Best for: WordPress users • All-in-one privacy suite • Consent statistics

€59 – €399/yr depending on sites

A privacy suite for WordPress: cookie banner, privacy policy generator, data processing agreements, cookie declarations. 1M+ installs.

✅ Pros: All-in-one (cookie consent + legal docs) • 1M+ active installs • Cookie scan included • Consent statistics dashboard
❌ Cons: WordPress-only — does not work on Shopify, Webflow, Next.js, or any other platform • No IAB TCF support • Upgrades auto-renew on sessions, no downgrade
Verdict: Great if you're on WordPress and want everything in one plugin. Doesn't work outside WordPress — which is why we built EUComply.
