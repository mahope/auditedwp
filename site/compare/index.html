What each option is actually best for

EUComply — best for: quick compliance checks and continuous monitoring on a budget

  • ✔ You need a free, instant, no-commitment check of any website
  • ✔ You run a small-to-medium business and want daily monitoring under $100/yr
  • ✔ You're a freelancer or agency checking multiple client sites for baseline compliance

EUComply is automated technical scanning. It finds signal — missing headers, absent consent banners, legal page gaps — in seconds. It does not replace a qualified legal or security audit, but it tells you exactly where to look and gives you the fix steps.

Manual audit — best for: legal defensibility and complex cases

  • ✔ You need a legally defensible audit for a regulator, investor, or insurer
  • ✔ Your site handles large volumes of personal data or is in a high-risk sector (health, finance)
  • ✔ You want human judgment on edge cases no automated tool can interpret

The con: cost and time. A proper GDPR audit starts around $500 and can take weeks. For a quick baseline, run a free scan first — you might find the auditor charges for things an automated check already catches.

Cookie scanners (Cookiebot, CookieYes, etc.) — best for: consent banner compliance only

  • ✔ You need a working consent banner with cookie categorization
  • ✔ You want automatic cookie discovery on your own site

They're narrowly focused — they don't check security headers, legal pages, NIS2, DORA, or EAA. If all you need is a cookie banner, use one. If you need broader compliance coverage, EUComply covers everything in one scan.

Enterprise platforms (OneTrust, Secureframe, Drata) — best for: large organizations with compliance teams

  • ✔ You have a dedicated compliance officer or team
  • ✔ You need SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other framework support alongside EU regulation
  • ✔ You have the budget ($10,000+/yr) and the headcount to configure and maintain them

These are powerful but dramatically overbuilt for a typical SMB. EUComply gives you the same core checks (GDPR, NIS2, DORA, EAA) without the integration project or the price tag.
