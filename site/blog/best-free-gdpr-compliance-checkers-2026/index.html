Why you need a compliance scanner

EU regulators have automated their own enforcement. The CNIL (France), DPC (Ireland), and ICO (UK) all use automated tools to scan public websites for basic compliance gaps. The most common triggers for fines are:

  • No cookie consent banner — ePrivacy Directive, fined up to €20M
  • Trackers loading before consent — the #1 source of GDPR fines against SMBs in 2025-2026
  • Missing privacy policy link on forms — GDPR Art. 13, automated enforcement since 2021
  • Weak HTTPS security — potential NIS2 supply-chain requirement

A free scanner gives you a 10-second first pass. If it flags issues, you know what to fix — and what to escalate to a lawyer.

What a free scanner cannot do

Free compliance scanners have limits. They are not legal advice and cannot replace a qualified data protection review. Specifically, free scanners cannot:

  • Read your privacy policy text — they detect whether a privacy policy link exists, but cannot verify that the content is legally correct for your jurisdiction
  • Verify cookie banner behaviour — they detect whether a consent banner is present, but cannot test whether the reject button works correctly or whether trackers stop firing after rejection (that requires a real browser session)
  • Check internal data processing — they scan public-facing pages only. Data processing agreements, employee training records, and data mapping are not visible from an external URL
  • Confirm legal compliance — compliance is a legal determination that depends on your specific data processing, jurisdiction, and business model. Automated checks are a diagnostic tool, not a certification

Use free scanners as a first pass — they tell you if something is obviously wrong. For formal compliance, combine automated scanning with a qualified legal review.

Do free scanners store my data?

EUComply does not store scanned URLs, results, or IPs — everything is processed in memory and discarded. Always check each tool's privacy policy before scanning sensitive domains.

What's the difference between a free scanner and a paid one?

Free scanners give you a point-in-time snapshot. Paid tools (like EUComply Pro, $79/yr) add continuous monitoring, PDF reports, DPA generators, NIS2 clause kits, EAA accessibility statements, and email alerts — useful for agencies, SaaS companies, and anyone who needs documented proof of compliance.

Can I scan my competitors' websites?

Technically yes — free scanners work on any public URL. However, scanning a website you do not own may violate the scanner's terms of service. Always check terms before scanning third-party domains.

All free scanners work on WordPress since they check public HTML. EUComply is the best choice because it also has a free WordPress plugin that adds WordPress-specific checks (backup status, plugin health, core version) — but the online scanner works the same way on any platform.
