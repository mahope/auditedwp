Cookiebot Alternative 2026: Why Site Owners Are Switching

August 24, 2026 · 8 min read · Compare EUComply vs Cookiebot →

In August 2025, Cookiebot (now owned by Usercentrics) sent a single email to its customers: pricing would double across all tiers, effective in 30 days. No grandfathering. No new features. Accounts with fewer than four domains saw their monthly bill jump from roughly €15 to €30 per domain — a 100% increase.

This was not a small adjustment. It was a pricing event that sent thousands of site owners, agency operators, and compliance teams looking for alternatives. And the conversation is still going in 2026 — Reddit threads, Trustpilot reviews, and comparison articles continue to rank for "Cookiebot alternative" because the switch has been slow and painful for many.

If you're evaluating alternatives in 2026, either because of the price hike or because Cookiebot's per-domain billing doesn't fit your setup anymore, this guide covers what the market looks like right now.

The 3 Types of Cookiebot Alternatives

Not everyone needs the same thing from a Cookiebot replacement. It helps to separate the alternatives into three categories:

1. Consent Management Platforms (CMPs)

These are direct Cookiebot replacements: they provide a cookie consent banner, manage consent, scan cookies, and integrate with IAB TCF and Google Consent Mode. The best-known options:

  • CookieYes ($10-20/mo) — Strong WordPress plugin, simple UI, good for single-site SMBs
  • iubenda (~€27/yr per site) — Cheapest per-site, 1,600+ legal clauses, includes policy generator
  • Termly ($10/mo) — All-in-one: policy generator + cookie banner
  • ConsentPixel ($8.99/domain/mo) — US-first with CIPA coverage

2. Compliance Scanners (Monitoring-first)

These don't replace your CMP banner — they monitor your site for compliance gaps across GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and EAA. They catch what a cookie-only tool misses:

  • EUComply (free / $79/yr) — Universal compliance scanner. Checks HTTPS, consent platforms, forms, legal pages, security headers, Google Consent Mode v2, IAB TCF, trackers, DORA resilience, and NIS2 signals. Free tier has no limits. Pro ($79/yr) adds daily monitoring and PDF reports.

3. All-in-One Privacy Platforms

Enterprise-grade platforms that combine consent, DSAR automation, policy generation, and vendor management:

  • OneTrust ($350+/mo) — The enterprise standard
  • Osano ($199/mo) — Includes "No-Fines" guarantee
  • Enzuzo ($59/mo flat for 10 domains) — DSAR automation included

Who Should Switch — and Who Should Stay

Switch if:

  • Your Cookiebot bill doubled and you manage 1-3 domains
  • You're paying per-domain and it doesn't scale
  • You need broader compliance coverage than just cookies (NIS2, DORA, EAA)
  • You want predictable flat-rate pricing

Stay with Cookiebot if:

  • You're on a grandfathered plan that hasn't changed
  • You need Google CMP Partner certification (Cookiebot is Gold certified)
  • You're deeply integrated with their CMP API and consent database

The Smartest Setup in 2026

The most common setup we see among site owners who switched is a two-tool approach:

  1. A lightweight CMP (CookieYes, iubenda, or Termly) for the cookie consent banner, consent database, and IAB TCF compliance — $10-30/mo
  2. A universal compliance scanner (EUComply) for ongoing monitoring of HTTPS, legal pages, security headers, NIS2, DORA, EAA, trackers, and consent platforms — free for one-off scans, $79/yr for daily monitoring

Why this works: the CMP handles consent (which is what regulators fine for), and the scanner catches everything else before an auditor or regulator does. Together they cost less than Cookiebot alone.

What to Consider Before Switching

Integration costs

Replacing a CMP requires updating your cookie banner script on every page. For compliance scanners like EUComply, there's nothing to install — just enter a URL. That makes scanners the easiest alternative to try.

Multi-domain management

Cookiebot charges per domain. Most alternatives do too (flat rate or bundled). EUComply Pro covers unlimited domains at the same $79/year price — the only compliance scanner with truly unlimited domain monitoring.

Regulation coverage

Cookiebot covers cookie consent and GDPR/CCPA. It doesn't cover NIS2 (effective 2024), DORA (effective January 2025), or the European Accessibility Act (effective June 2025). If your site serves EU users, these regulations apply — and they require more than a cookie banner.
